# Review 1 — PRJ_666

## Title
Climate Change Impact Assessment and Environmental Risk Prediction Platform

## Problem
Environmental information is often distributed across datasets and difficult to translate into timely operational decisions. A digital platform is needed to process climate indicators, assess impact and predict environmental risk.

## Objectives
1. Process climate/environmental data efficiently.
2. Assess climate impact using transparent indicators.
3. Predict energy/environmental risk using AI.
4. Detect anomalous environmental patterns.
5. Provide alerts and recommendations.
6. Apply basic cybersecurity controls.
7. Provide a cloud-ready architecture.
8. Support SDG 13 — Climate Action.

## Modules
Data ingestion, Big Data analytics, impact assessment, AI prediction, anomaly detection, decision support, cybersecurity, dashboard and cloud deployment.

## Expected outcome
A working web platform demonstrating data-to-decision workflow.
